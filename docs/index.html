Home Remote Support

Get your phone connected to a compatible LG webOS TV.

Connect to your TV

  1. Turn on the TV and put your phone on the same home network.
  2. Open Home Remote, allow local network access and tap Find my TV.
  3. Select your TV. Confirm the selected TV in the app and approve pairing on the TV using your usual remote.

Each phone pairs separately. The app uses its saved pairing when the TV is reachable again.

If your TV does not appear

Check Home Remote’s Local Network permission on iPhone or its Nearby Devices permission where available on Android. Use your main home network if the guest network separates connected devices. You can enter the TV’s local address in Advanced diagnostics when discovery is unavailable.

Compatibility

Home Remote is designed for compatible LG TVs running webOS. Earlier Android builds were tested with an LG OLED65G26LA (G2). Remote controls, playback, keyboards, app shortcuts and wake availability vary by model, firmware and the active TV app. Keep your usual remote for setup and recovery.

Get help

For setup questions or to report a problem, open a support issue. Include the Home Remote version, phone and TV models, and the step that failed. Public issues can be read by others. Keep addresses, access codes, credentials and contact information out of the report.
