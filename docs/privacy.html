Home Remote Privacy Policy

This policy covers Home Remote distributed through Google Play and the App Store.

TV control and data on your phone

Home Remote uses local network access to find and control the TV you select. Remote commands and any text you choose to send from the TV keyboard go to that TV. Home Remote has no account, advertising, analytics or cloud control service. A streaming app opened on the TV handles its own data under that service’s policies.

Secure connections are the default for supported LG webOS TVs. If you enable a legacy connection option, some commands may travel unencrypted on your home network.

Home Remote keeps saved TV profiles, pairing credentials, trusted connection information and preferences on the phone. Pairing credentials use the phone’s protected storage. Current playback and TV app status are used to display controls and are not kept as a viewing history.

Removing saved data

Use Forget TV to remove a saved profile and its pairing from this phone. This does not remove a profile from another phone or clear the TV’s own list of approved remotes. The TV’s settings manage its approved remotes.

Diagnostics and support

Home Remote prepares a diagnostic report locally. It leaves the app only when you choose to copy or share it. If you contact support, the support channel receives the details you decide to provide so the publisher can respond. Public support issues are visible to others.

Store purchases and updates

Google Play and the App Store handle the download, payment, receipts and updates. Home Remote does not request your payment details in the app.

Contact

For a privacy question, use the contact options on the Home Remote support page. Ask for a private reply before sharing personal information.
